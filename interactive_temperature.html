About the Weather Data

ERA5 Reanalysis Data

This application uses ERA5, the fifth generation of European Centre for Medium-Range Weather Forecasts (ECMWF) atmospheric reanalysis data. ERA5 provides comprehensive, high-quality weather information by combining:

  • Historical observations from weather stations, satellites, and other sources worldwide
  • Advanced climate models that use the laws of physics to fill gaps in observations
  • Global coverage on a 31km grid with hourly data from 1940 to present

Why ERA5? It's considered the gold standard for climate reanalysis, providing consistent, quality-assured weather data that's used by researchers, meteorologists, and climate scientists worldwide.

Appearent Temperature (UTCI)

The UTCI (Universal Thermal Climate Index) measures how hot or cold it actually feels to the human body, not just the air temperature. It accounts for:

  • Air temperature - The basic temperature measurement
  • Humidity - How moisture affects heat perception
  • Wind speed - Cooling effect of air movement
  • Solar radiation - Heat from direct sunlight

UTCI is expressed as an equivalent temperature (°C) that represents how the human body physiologically responds to these combined weather conditions. For example, 35°C air temperature with high humidity and no wind might feel like 42°C UTCI.

UTCI Comfort Categories: No thermal stress (9-26°C), Moderate heat stress (32-38°C), Strong heat stress (38-46°C), and Extreme heat stress (above 46°C).
